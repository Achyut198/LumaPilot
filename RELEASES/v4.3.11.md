# LumaPilot v4.3.11

- Tracks each known display by its stable hardware identity (vendor/model/serial) instead of its transient display ID, so a monitor reconnected under a new ID is recognized as connected and never shown as "off" after sleep/wake, reconnect, GPU switch, or a disable/enable cycle.
- Removes the manual "Clear disconnected displays" action in favor of automatic self-healing: stale and duplicate display references are reconciled and pruned on their own.
- Records a display's identity before it is turned off, so a disabled display can always be listed and turned back on again — it can no longer become invisible or unrecoverable.
- Rejects phantom/empty-name display references created during reconfiguration, fixing the blank "ghost" row in the menu and the shortcut ordering it broke.
- Adds an automatic switch to the built-in display when turning off your only remaining external display, so you are never left with a black screen.
